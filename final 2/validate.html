Grant Application Intake

Four fields, all required. Nothing is accepted until every one of them passes, and the form tells you exactly which one failed.

At least 2 characters.

Needs an @ and a dot after it, e.g. owner@shop.com

Whole dollars between 1,000 and 1,000,000. No commas or dollar sign.

How I tested it

Same way I tested a circuit: feed it the bad input on purpose. Empty name. Email with no @ sign. Email with an @ but no dot after it. Amount of 500, then 2,000,000, then 12.50, then the word "lots". No grant type selected. Every one of those has to be caught, and the message has to name the field that failed, or the check is no good.
