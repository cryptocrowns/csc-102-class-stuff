Palindrome Checker

A palindrome reads the same forward and backward. Spaces, capitals, and punctuation are ignored, so "A man, a plan, a canal: Panama" counts.

Letters and numbers are compared. Everything else is stripped out.

How it works

Four steps, decided before any code was written. Strip out everything that is not a letter or a number. Force what is left to lower case. Reverse that cleaned copy. Compare the reversed copy to the cleaned original. If they match, it is a palindrome.
